# ⚖️ Copyright, Ownership & Responsible AI Voice Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

## 1. Ownership of Generated Audio & Voiceovers
- **Full Creator Ownership:** As the user of VoiceStudio, you retain 100% full ownership, intellectual property rights, and copyright in all voiceover scripts you submit and all synthesized audio output files generated using this platform.
- **Commercial Rights:** Subject to compliance with voice consent obligations (Section 2), you are free to use, distribute, broadcast, monetize, and publish generated audio files across YouTube, podcasts, audiobooks, advertisements, and social media without royalty obligations to VoiceStudio.
- **Zero Claim on User Content:** Neither the creators nor contributors of VoiceStudio claim any copyright, license fees, or ownership over your output files or trained reference profiles.

---

## 2. Voice Consent & Ethical AI Policy
Voice cloning technology must be used ethically and legally. By using VoiceStudio, you agree to adhere strictly to the following standards:

1. **Explicit Consent Required:**
   - You must have explicit, informed permission from any living individual before cloning their voice.
   - For your own voice, you retain full rights to create and monetize voice models.
2. **Prohibited Activities:**
   - **Fraud & Social Engineering:** Generating voice clones to impersonate family members, bank officials, corporate executives, or authority figures.
   - **Deceptive Media & Defamation:** Creating misleading deepfake news, altered political speeches, or defamatory audio intended to harm someone's reputation.
   - **Harassment:** Generating non-consensual voice messages for intimidation, hate speech, or harassment.
3. **Disclosure & Transparency:**
   - When distributing cloned voiceovers in public broadcasts, documentaries, or news contexts, we strongly recommend including an ethical attribution disclaimer (e.g., *"Voice simulated using AI technology with permission"*).

---

## 3. Third-Party Samples & Fair Use
- If you use reference samples of public figures, speakers, or celebrities for research, educational parody, or personal testing, you must comply with applicable local laws regarding Right of Publicity, Fair Use, and trademark law.
- Using a celebrity's voice to endorse products, services, or commercial businesses without their express endorsement agreement violates Right of Publicity laws in many jurisdictions.

## 4. Open Source Terms
VoiceStudio is built as free, community-oriented software. Developers are welcome to inspect, customize, and extend the tool for their own audio workflows. Users are responsible for ensuring that their projects comply with applicable local audio, copyright, and privacy laws.
